Delete your data

Last updated 13 November 2026

How to delete your account and everything you posted.

In the app

Open Home → My account → Delete account, and confirm. This immediately and permanently removes your account, reports (including anonymous ones you posted), comments, Discussion messages, assistant chats, photos, videos and notification settings.

Without the app

Write to <YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL> from the email address of your account and ask us to delete it. We will do so within 30 days and confirm by email.
